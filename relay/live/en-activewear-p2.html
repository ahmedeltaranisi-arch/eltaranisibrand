Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my size?

Please refer to our size guide and the fit notes on each product page for specific recommendations. If you need help finding your ideal size, email us at care@eltaranisibrand.com and we’ll be happy to assist.

What is Eltaranisi Brand’s return and exchange policy?

We gladly accept returns and exchanges on items not marked Final Sale within 30 days of purchase. Items must be in their original condition—unworn, unwashed, and with tags attached. To start a return or exchange, visit our Returns page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide through our trusted partner, Global-e, via DHL Express.

  • Orders under $200 USD: $15 USD flat rate
  • Orders over $200 USD: Free shipping

International orders are typically processed within 2-3 business days and delivered within 2-5 business days after shipping. Please note that customs may occasionally cause additional delays beyond our control.

Amounts shown reflect the USD equivalent in your local currency.

What makes Eltaranisi Brand’s fabrics different?

We thoughtfully source natural fibers and hold our materials to higher standards for environmental and social responsibility. Many organic fabrications are certified to GOTS™ and Regenerative Organic Certified™ standards, while OEKO-TEX® certification ensures select materials are tested for harmful substances. Beyond our fabrics, Eltaranisi Brand is The Climate Label certified and a Certified B Corp—part of our ongoing commitment to making better choices at every step.
